#

Tally — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

In short: Tally does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you create in the app — counters, folders, presets, sessions, history, and settings — stays on your device.

Data Collection

Tally does not collect any personal information. The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies of any kind.

Data Storage

All content you create in Tally — counters, folders, presets, session data, history, theme and accent color preferences, and app icon selection — is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to any server operated by us or by any third party.

Backups & Portable Sync

Tally's backup and portable sync features let you export your own data to a file for your own safekeeping or to move it between your own devices. These files are created and handled entirely by you (for example, via the Files app, AirDrop, or a cloud storage service of your choosing); we do not have access to them.

Third-Party Services

Tally does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing services.

Children's Privacy

Tally does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because the app does not collect information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future — for example, if an optional iCloud sync feature is added — this page will be updated to reflect exactly what data is involved and how it's handled, and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
